# Tutorial — the retired "Before you start" section

Removed from `host/tutorial.md` because it stood between a first-time reader and their first
sound: four blocks of preamble, a homework link to the README, and a demonstration of the
tutorial's own machinery, all before anything had been heard. Kept here because parts of it
still say something worth saying, and may return in some other form.

The section's contents-list has already moved into the tutorial proper, renumbered. Everything
below is the text exactly as it was when it was cut.

## What would need fixing before any of it is reused

- **The Show-me paragraph is wrong in three ways.** It calls the marker "this symbol" when it is
  now an orange rounded square with a question mark; it says changing section clears the ring,
  which is no longer how the continuous-scroll tutorial works; and it omits the actual rule,
  which is that scrolling the marker out of view clears the ring. A corrected one-line version
  of it now lives at the head of the contents list in `host/tutorial.md`.
- **"DR for short"** is introduced here and used throughout the rest of the tutorial. With this
  section gone the abbreviation appears unexplained; the remaining uses should be spelled out,
  or the abbreviation reintroduced wherever it now first occurs.
- **The dark-mode extension warning** is real and has no other home. If browser extensions
  recolouring the panels turn out to bite people, it wants somewhere better than a section
  nobody reads before they have heard anything — a first-run notice, or the README.

## The text, as it was

**Before you start,** please turn off any browser extension that recolours pages — Dark Reader and the like — for this site. DreamRack (DR for short) has its own light and dark modes, and those extensions distort its panels.

**This tutorial** is divided into sections that progressively cover what you need to know to get started with the instrument. Its window stays open, so you never have to dismiss it to follow it — drag it anywhere by its title and resize if needed. Marked blocks are your cue to act, like this one:

> **Do this** — Read the [README](https://github.com/chrisgr99/DreamRack/blob/main/README.md) if you haven't — it covers what DR is, which this tutorial won't repeat.

**Show me:** wherever the tutorial names something on screen, it may carry this symbol {see:complexOsc259t#title} — click it and that thing is ringed in orange, with a line drawn from the symbol to it. Click the same symbol again to clear the ring, or click another to move it; only one is shown at a time, and changing section clears it. The rack stays live underneath, so you can work a control while it is ringed.

> **Do this** — Click the symbol in the paragraph above. The "Complex Oscillator" title bar is ringed, with a line running to it. Click it again to clear.
